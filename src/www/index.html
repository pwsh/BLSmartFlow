Fan curve

Map the source temperature to a fan speed. Drag the points, edit the table, or start from a preset - then save to the device.

Curve

Drag a point to move it, click the line to insert one, Delete removes the selected point. Values snap to 1 °C / 1 %. The dashed marker shows the live source temperature and the resulting output.

Network

WiFi credentials for the device itself. Saving always requires a restart.

Access point mode - the device is not on your WiFi yet.You are connected to the device's own hotspot. Pick your network below, enter the password and save. The device restarts, joins your WiFi, and this hotspot disappears.
Saved - the device is restarting.Reconnect your phone or computer to your normal WiFi, then open http://blsmartflow.local/. If that name does not resolve, look up the device's new IP address in your router.
